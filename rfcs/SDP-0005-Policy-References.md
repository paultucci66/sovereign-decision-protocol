# SDP-0005: Policy References

Status: Draft Skeleton

Version: 0.1

Author: Paul Tucci

License: Apache 2.0

## Abstract

This RFC will define how SDP Decision Objects reference policies, rules, standards, contracts, controls, or other governing authorities.

Knowledge may inform.

Policy authorizes.

## Purpose

The purpose of this RFC is to ensure decisions can cite the policy basis under which they were made.

## Scope

This RFC is expected to define:

- Policy reference format
- Policy version references
- Rule/key references
- Policy source references
- Policy authority metadata
- Policy effective dates
- Policy expiration
- Supersession
- Policy citation requirements

## Non-Goals

This RFC will not define:

- Policy authoring systems
- Policy engines
- Policy storage
- Policy-as-code implementation
- Retrieval mechanisms
- Vector search
- Governance workflows for changing policy

## Design Note

Policies used for enforcement should be exact, versioned, attributable, and reproducible.

Fuzzy retrieval may inform understanding.

It should not authorize a consequential decision.

## Open Questions

- What policy metadata is required for conformance?
- Should policy citations include rule keys?
- How should policy conflicts be represented?
- How should superseded policies be referenced years later?
